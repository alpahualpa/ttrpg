Services at this long-standing sea god’s temple are well attended. The congregation is led by a one-legged former whaler: [[Ghosts of Saltmarsh/NPC/Saltmarsh/Wellgar Brinehanded|Wellgar Brinehanded]]. Matters ashore rarely interest him, but the temple and its bell tower are also served by a half-dozen novitiates and laypeople who keep things running smoothly.
