Bearing the dubious honor of being the oldest tavern in town, the Wicker Goat is owned by [[master_data_saltmarsh/NPCs/Regular NPCs/Lankus Kurrid]]. The two-story building has sleeping quarters for rent on the upper floor, usually sufficient to accommodate the slow stream of travelers making their way through [[Ghosts of Saltmarsh/Locations/Saltmarsh/Saltmarsh|Saltmarsh]] on the way to somewhere else.
