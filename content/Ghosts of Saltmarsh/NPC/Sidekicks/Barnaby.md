***Description***
Barnaby is a town mad prophet who hears "the Foam" and "the Lady of Tides". Bold head, a beard shaped like salt icicles, dirty robe and a "mixed" scent - that's him. If you hear the small bell ringing - that's Barnaby's staff, he's using it for his "magic".

For players:
Barnaby is your spellcaster ally that can help in a difficult situation.
