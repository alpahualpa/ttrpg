Late in the night, [[Aisen]] lingered in **[[The Snapping Line]]**, Saltmarsh’s rough tavern by the harbor. Before the hour grew too late, the village’s familiar mad prophet, **[[Barnaby]]**, burst in and dragged **[[Rey the Herm]]** by the elbow, crying out that something foul was stirring on the cliff.

> “Something is rotting on the cliff, as the Lady of Tides said!”

His warning sent the party racing toward the **[[Ghosts of Saltmarsh/Locations/Saltmarsh/Saltmarsh Cemetery|Saltmarsh Cemetery]]**.
### The Cemetery Gates

At the cemetery, **[[Wellgar Brinehanded]]**, priest of Procan, had drawn a protective ritual circle across the gates. Town guards stood watch beside him, uneasy but resolute.

As the players approached, something began battering the gates from within.

The guards broke and fled.

Then the dead came crashing through.

A horde of skeletons burst from the cemetery and knocked Wellgar unconscious in the chaos. The party fought them back and managed to revive the fallen priest. In the aftermath, they discovered a troubling loss: someone had stolen the priest’s scrolls, including the scroll of **Raise Dead**.
### The Old Crypt of the Owelands

The source of the undead uprising was revealed to be powerful necromancy emanating from the old crypt of the **Oweland family**. Rather than charging headlong into the depths, the party chose a harsher and smarter answer.

They brought **oil, wood, and fire**.

The crypt was destroyed in a roaring collapse of flame and stone. The dark mage within was buried alive beneath the ruin. He nearly escaped to the surface, but Aisen struck him hard with a shield and drove him back into the fire.

The crypt fell silent.
### What Was Found

When the smoke cleared, only a hand of mage remained beneath the mud and broken stone, still clutching a purse.

Inside were:

- a potion,
- a note,
- and a ring set with a large red stone.

The note carried instructions from the mage’s masters, along with a suspicious code.
### Session End State

- Wellgar Brinehanded: Alive, but knocked out during the attack
- Skeletons: Destroyed
- Oweland crypt: Destroyed by fire and collapse
- Dark mage: Buried and likely dead
- Loot recovered: Potion, note, ring with red stone
- New lead: Strange code

The midnight ended with the players looking out into the dark sea.

And there, far offshore, the same series of signals appeared again.
